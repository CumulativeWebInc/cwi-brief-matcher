How scoring works

All scoring runs in your browser, client-side. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  1. Your brief is lowercased and split into words.
  2. Each word earns 3 points per exact match against a track's editorial mood tags.
  3. Each word earns 2 points per exact match against a word in the track title.
  4. Each word also earns 2 points per mood tag reached through the synonym map below.
  5. Tracks are ranked by total score; ties keep catalog order.

Scores are an algorithmic estimate — UNVERIFIED, not human curation.
